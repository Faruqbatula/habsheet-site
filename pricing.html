What you are actually buying

Not the pad. A pad is under a pound. What costs you is everything that happens after the van drives off — the scribbled notes, the writing up, the scanning, the email that goes out two days later.

The report leaves with the customer

Filled in, signed and handed over on the driveway, rather than typed up at the kitchen table that evening.

Nothing is written twice

Your details, the client, the van, the meter, the conditions — all carried in before you start. You enter what you found, and nothing else.

The van's history is there next year

Last year's readings sit on the same diagram, in the same places. A panel creeping up is something you see rather than something in a folder.

Nothing is lost

Every reading is written to the device as you take it. It holds up to being pulled out of a filing cabinet a year later, which is when it matters.

Questions

Can I try it before I pay?

Yes. Every plan starts with a free trial on your own device, with no card. If it does not suit how you work, it costs you nothing.

My season is only eight months. Am I paying for the winter?

On monthly billing you can stop and start whenever you like, and your jobs stay where they are. Annual billing is cheaper if you work all year.

What happens to my jobs if I stop paying?

They stay readable and exportable. You cannot start new ones until you subscribe again, but nothing you have already done is taken away from you.

The sheets get revised. Who keeps up with that?

We do, on every plan, at no extra cost. When a sheet is revised you are working from the new one, rather than finding out from a customer.

Two of us work out of the same workshop. Which plan?

Business. One library of clients and vehicles that everybody sees, rather than two tablets that know nothing about each other.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Everything happens on the tablet. A driveway with no bars is the normal case, not the exception.

What does "Soon" mean?

It is being built now and it is included in the plan it sits in when it arrives. You will not be asked for more money for it.
