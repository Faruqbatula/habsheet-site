Questions

My season is only eight months. Am I paying for the winter?

On monthly billing you can stop and start whenever you like, and your jobs stay where they are. Annual billing is cheaper if you work all year.

What happens to my jobs if my subscription ends?

Every job you’ve done stays on your device, and you can still open, export and share all of them, for good. Paperwork you have finished is never taken away. Starting new jobs and editing stop 30 days after your subscription ends, so nothing is left half-finished.

The sheets get revised. Who keeps up with that?

We do, at no extra cost. When a sheet is revised you are working from the new one, rather than finding out from a customer.

Two of us work out of the same workshop. What do we need?

Business: one workshop, up to five engineers, sharing clients, vehicles and jobs under one subscription, for £950 a year. It opens with Pro. Its shared workshop features are marked Coming above; until they arrive, each engineer keeps their own clients and vehicles.

Does it work without a signal?

Yes. Everything happens on the device. A driveway with no bars is the normal case, not the exception.
