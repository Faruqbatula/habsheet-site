What it does

The sheets, damp readings on the diagram, saved drawings, the head of the sheet, and the export.

The sheets

Which sheets

The habitation service record and the damp report, for tourers — single and twin axle — motorhomes and campervans. The vehicle is chosen when the job starts, and it decides which sheets the job uses.

Answers

Tap P, F, N/A or R in the row. Those are the four answers the sheet prints, and there are no others.

Readings

Tap a box and type the value. The unit is already printed beside the box, so a value typed with its unit is refused.

Observations

A written observation runs on to the next ruled line rather than being cut short.

Signatures

You and the customer sign in the signature boxes, on the tablet.

Saving

Every change is saved on the tablet as you make it, and can be undone.

Drag a crop sideways to read all of it.

Damp readings on the diagram

Taking readings

Set the value on the tape dial. Every tap on the drawing then puts that value where you tapped. Move the tape when the reading changes.

Values

Whole numbers, 0 to 100.

On screen

The page counts its readings by band — under 15, 15 to 20, 20 to 30, and 30 and above — and names the highest.

On paper

Readings are black, on the screen and on the page, like a pen.

Marking up

Lines, dashed lines, boxes, vents, circles, hatched areas and freehand pen, with notes written on the drawing.

Year on year

Each vehicle keeps its jobs, and its damp readings are compared with the last survey that had them.

Saved drawings per model

What is saved

The drawing — windows, lockers, vents and seams — named by make and model. Never the readings.

When it is used

A van of a model you have drawn before starts from that drawing, even if that van has never been in.

Last year’s job

A van you have seen before can start from its own previous drawing instead. The readings are always taken fresh.

The right vehicle

A tourer drawing is never offered for a motorhome, or the other way round.

The head of the sheet

Your images

Your workshop’s own images, placed at the head of the sheet.

Preview

The preview is laid out by the same code as the PDF, so what you arrange is what prints.

Every document

One arrangement goes on every document. You can look at it on each one, including the one with the least room.

Hide or remove

An image can be hidden without removing it, and shown again later.

The export

What you choose

Both documents as one PDF — the default, four pages — or the service record or the damp report on its own.

File name

Each file is named with the date, the document, the customer and the VIN.

Before it goes

Anything that will not fit its box is listed first, so you can shorten it. Nothing is cut off without you seeing it.

Sending it

Through the tablet’s own share options — email, messages, or saved to files. Making the PDF needs no signal.
