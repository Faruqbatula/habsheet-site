Habsheet

The habitation check, done on a tablet.

Habsheet lets caravan and motorhome habitation engineers complete the service check sheet and the damp report on screen, and hand the customer a finished PDF before leaving site.

What it does

  • The sheets you already use. Not a redesign — the paperwork your customers recognise, filled in on screen.
  • Records, rather than ticks. Voltages, trip times, continuity, tread depths, torque settings and expiry dates are entered as values.
  • Damp readings on the diagram. Tapped straight onto the vehicle, with the features drawn in.
  • Signed on site. Technician and customer signatures captured on the tablet.
  • One PDF for the customer. Both documents together, before you leave the driveway.
  • Year on year. Readings kept against the vehicle, so a van getting worse is visible rather than filed away.

Where it's up to

Habsheet is in testing with working engineers on real jobs, on iPad, iPhone and Android. If you carry out habitation checks and would like to try it, get in touch.
