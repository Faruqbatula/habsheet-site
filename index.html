Who it is for

Engineers who carry out habitation checks on tourers, motorhomes and campervans.

What you do

Tap the answer box on the sheet. Type readings into their boxes. Put damp readings on the drawing where you took them. You and the customer sign on the tablet.

What it keeps

Your details, your clients and their vehicles, and a drawing for each make and model you have marked up before.

What it produces

The service record and the damp report, as one four-page PDF or each on its own.

Signal

Not needed. Everything happens on the tablet.

What it does, in detail

On the tablet

The sheet itself, answered on screen, and the tape dial for damp readings. Screens from the app on a 12.9-inch iPad, with a sample job.

Tourer, motorhome, campervan

Each vehicle has its own drawings, and each reading goes where it was taken. These are from three sample reports.
